Summary of Club Meeting on Wednesday 24th April

Max made some good progress on changing the colour of the ball in his ping pong game. He sparked some interest in Stoo and Igor when he wanted to go further and have the colour change gradually over time. There's been quite a few posts on our Discord server and we now have at least one solution.

Tomasz merged his code into a FastApi template and is now working on creating a UI, user interface, to ask the user to select which metrics, date ranges and plot type for his UK weather application which uses the met office data.

Aleks made some progress with his space invaders game. He learnt how to scale images to a size appropriate for having several alien spaceships on a line. One of the next steps would be to make them move.

Natan fine 'tuned' his SDR,  software defined radio, program which he'll be using in his flight simulator project.

Bill is still going strong with his unity game called Darcy. Stoo has been working on getting it sunning on his own PC and is   integrating it with his Oculus VR headset. It's nice to see that our club members are motivating us to join in.

All in all, a very good meeting.
