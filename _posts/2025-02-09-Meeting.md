It's been a while since I've posted anything about our Code Club,  we've had quite a lot of illness and work commitments that has meant we've had to cancel some meetings. However, we are still meeting regularly at  Curborough Community Centre thanks to the kind support of Ania and the other regulars.

We met on Wednesday 5th February and our members are, mostly, focusing on learning Unity. We would encourage the parents of our members, and other who may be interested, to take a look at the Unity Essentials course they are working thorough and ask them to show what they've learnt so far. https://learn.unity.com/pathway/unity-essentials?version=6

Besides learning Unity, another project we've just embarked on, with Natan leading the way, is to make a Ceilometer, which is used to measure cloud height. It's based on some older projects which we will modernise to use a Raspberry Pi Pico. https://chrisramsay.co.uk/posts/2014/03/cloud-detection-part-one and https://www.hackster.io/KFMAKR/ceilometer-3339f0.
We've ordered the components, the kit from PiHut has already arrived.

Don't forget that we meet every Wednesday at 5:30pm at Curborough Community Centre. Below is a photo from our last meeting showing that it's a quiet place to write programs, get support from club members and two STEM ambassadors. On that note, please know that both Stewart and I are not teachers and have worked in the IT sector, for more decades than we care to admit,  and so can bring real life experience and knowledge to our members.

Hope that each of our members, and followers, have a lovely weekend and hope to see you soon.
