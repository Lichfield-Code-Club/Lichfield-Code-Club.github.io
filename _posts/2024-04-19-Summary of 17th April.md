Summary of 17th April Club Meeting

A really good meeting.

Stoo and Natan were looking further into SDR, software defined radio, in relation to Natan's flight simulation project and were able to get a clear signal by the end of the evening.

Bill's work on his Unity game has motivated Stoo to install Unity and Bill's game on his own PC with the intent of integrating it with his Occulus VR headset.

Max made good progress on his ping pong game. Stoo was able to test his understanding to make sure it wasn't just copy 'n' paste and he's really pleased with what Max was able to describe.

Aleks investigated the scaling of a large alien image and found out what happens when the PC runs low on memory, so we're looking into creating a range of scaled images as separate assets so that the game engine doesn't need to do any software scaling.

Tomasz, great progress, was able to install the various python modules to start using FastAPi. This working is getting really exciting and all the more valuable because it's a part of his Duke of Edinburgh award.
