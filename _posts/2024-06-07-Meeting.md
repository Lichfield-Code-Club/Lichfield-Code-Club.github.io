Summary of Club Meeting on Wednesday 5th June

Sounds like everyone had a good half term break and were keen to make progress on their projects.

Bill added some interesting physics to his Unity platform game. When the character jumped onto a conveyor belt it immediately started moving faster and if he jumped up and faced the opposite way to the motion of the belt the speed was clearly reduced. Great work Bill

Natan was working on "ethical" hacking. He has a friend who had created a website and wanted Natan to see how secure it was. Unfortunately it wasn't very secure because the api (access) key had been stored in a cookie which could be used to wreak havoc with the website.

Max was working on his Liverpool Football Club website. He updated several images and learnt how to resize them whilst also keeping the aspect ratio the same. It was nice to see Igor helping him out too. It's good to share thoughts.

Tomasz, and I, struggled to extend his graph plot app to draw Pi charts and not just histograms. I'll spend some time figuring out how it's done before the next club meeting.

Aleks worked on adding animations to his space invaders game and seemed to be making good progress.

Igor has taken an interest in some lidar based landscape simulations.
https://youtube.com/shorts/BxdQmoDNg2A?feature=shared
It'll be interesting to see how it progresses.
